The new budgeting article is the operating-system version of that insight. Stop allocating capital to last year's segments. Reallocate to the ten to twenty percent of cells with disproportionate return. The two pieces are almost twenty years apart but they describe the same physics. Capital follows opportunity at the cell level. The annual budget is the institutional friction that prevents it.

If you see Warsh deliver a unified press conference on June seventeenth, the term premium narrows. If you see daylight between governors in their post-FOMC speeches the following week, the term premium widens. Either way, that is a tradable read.
